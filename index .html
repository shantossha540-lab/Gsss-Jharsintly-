ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Building a Better Future Through Education

GSSS Jharsintly is a Government Senior Secondary School committed to providing quality education and creating a positive learning environment for students.

The school provides education from Nursery to Class 12 and offers Science, Commerce and Arts streams at the senior secondary level.
